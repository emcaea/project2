Flightfeather and the Guide

Author: fluffykitten42

Moon: ???

Flightfeather stood in the grassy plains, waiting for the familiar figure to appear. The stars glittered above him, illuminating the strange landscape around him.

A fiery calico cat appeared before him, their starry pelt glowing against the darkness. Flightfeather turned toward his mysterious guide, anxious for answers.

"When will I get to see my brother?" Flightfeather asked, struggling to hide his frustration.

Rainstar had told him what he needed to do. He was supposed to become a mediator and sabotage the cats that StarClan didn't approve of. But the longer he waited, the more uncertain he became.

He still hadn't heard anything about his brother, and the silence was beginning to wear on him.

His guide asked him to be patient and give things more time, reminding him of the promise that his brother would return.

Flightfeather left the plains with his thoughts in turmoil. When he awoke, doubts continued to follow him. Was what he was doing truly right? He wasn't sure anymore, but he still needed them.
