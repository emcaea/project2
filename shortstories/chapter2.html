Chapter 2:Pale Yellow Eyes

Author: JustcallmeDawn

Moon: 9

Poppyfleck was sick when the strange cats brought her to RainClan. Weak and exhausted, she found herself resting in the medicine den, surrounded by unfamiliar scents and faces.

She wasn't sure what to think of RainClan or whether she truly belonged among them. Everything felt unfamiliar, leaving her uncertain about what would happen next.

One day, she noticed a pinkish-ginger tabby with white markings sitting on the sunlit rocks.

Their eyes met for a brief moment, but something about the encounter left Poppyfleck shaken.

Even afterward, she couldn't quite forget the moment. There was something about that gaze that lingered in her thoughts.

Before long, Poppyfleck began to feel as though she was being watched. The feeling was unsettling, especially when she thought about those familiar pale yellow eyes.
