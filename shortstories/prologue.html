Prologue: Shadebloom and Rainwatcher

Author: JustcallmeDawn

Moon: -21

A young, long-furred she pattered towards a small group of cats who were quietly socializing in a grassy clearing. The number of cats couldn't have been more than five, each within a few feet of each other. Their friendship was reflective in more than their close proximity; the warm smiles and ambiance could melt even the most stoic cat.

The long-furred she muffled through a mouthful of fresh-kill, "Shaeboom!"

In response to the call, a mostly white molly with a grey tail and head markings turned from her conversation with a much older grey bengal tom-cat.

The white feline flicked her tail in acknowledgement. "Well-done, RainWatcher," a soft purr rested at the end of ShadeBloom's sentence, "...for your first independent patrol, that's just fine."

RainWatcher's blue and cobalt eyes shined at the acknowledgement, her fur fluffing with pride. Before RainWatcher had the opportunity to respond, the bengal tom ShadeBloom had been talking with piped in.

"Welcome back RainWatcher, have you by chance seen MistKit? FlurryHawk was looking for her earlier and mentioned she'd been at the edge of camp."

RainWatcher rested the mouse down at her feet then looked up to the tom.

"No, I hadn't seen her when I came in..." She glanced back at the camp entrance. "...could she have left?"

ShadeBloom's ear twitched. "I don't believe she did... There are many places to hide here between the rocks and ridges. The kit may have found her way into the medicine den, IcePuddle..."

The bengal tom looked to the authoritative she. "...go look."

IcePuddle nodded sharply and walked towards a rocky tunnel in the west side of the clearing.

The other gathered felines noticed the change in atmosphere and all looked to ShadeBloom for explanation and direction, some with quizzical gazes and others with an excitement likely brought on by boredom.

The large she-cat quickly gave instructions to each feline, and they dispersed throughout the camp, all calling for MistKit.

RainWatcher observed in awe at the posture and strength the white molly exhibited. RainWatcher thought she would probably always remember the imprint of ShadeBloom's reliable figure amongst a Clan of unsure felines.

RainWatcher was a relatively small she-cat with marbled markings of interchanging blue-greys and blacks. While RainWatcher knew she was only 12 moons old and still had the opportunity to gain strength, in the light of cats such as ShadeBloom, she felt small regardless.

Eventually, though, she hopes that one day she can be a reliable leader.

A young, long-furred she pattered towards a small group of cats who were quietly socializing in a grassy clearing.
