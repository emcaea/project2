About RainClan

RainClan has struggled for moons with war, sickness, and in-fighting; only after many losses and pain, they’ve finally entered an era of peace. The war with CloudClan now settled and illnesses now just a forgotten memory; the leader, RainStar, and her clanmembers will do whatever they can to maintain their hard-earned rest. Only, they’ll have to learn to live with the grief left over from the past. Can they recover? Will their peace survive their harsh environment?
